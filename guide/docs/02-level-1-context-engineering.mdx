Start from the tag `level-0-start`, clean: the catch-up above puts you on your own branch `level-1` there, whatever Level 0 left behind. Levels 1 to 4 work through the columns of your Level 0 observation card.

## Steps

### 1. Generate a first CLAUDE.md

Run `/init`. Read the result critically and cross out everything Claude could have read from the code itself. Anthropic's own guidance: every line costs attention on every task, and the most useful line is the command that runs the tests.

### 2. Rewrite CLAUDE.md to at most 60 lines

Rewrite `CLAUDE.md` to at most 60 lines. It must contain:

- Build and test commands, for example:

  ```bash
  ./mvnw verify
  ./mvnw -pl bookstore test -Dtest=BookTest
  ```

- A definition of done: verify green, including `ArchitectureTest`; never say "tests pass" without showing output.
- The five rules an agent cannot infer quickly: domain is pure Java; money is `Money` (BigDecimal), never double; schema changes only via a *new* Flyway migration; inbound adapters only talk to flows; no Lombok.
- A path instead of an essay: "Read docs/architecture.md before adding or moving classes."

Compare that path with the import syntax `@docs/architecture.md`. An import loads the whole file at session start and costs context on every task. A plain path lets Claude read it only when needed. Try both and look at `/context`.

### 3. Add path-scoped rules

A path-scoped rule is a markdown file in `.claude/rules/` whose header names the files it is about. Create `.claude/rules/domain.md` with this exact content:

```markdown
---
paths:
  - "bookstore/src/main/java/**/domain/**"
---
# Domain layer
- Aggregates: private constructor, an intention-revealing static factory for creation, `restore(...)` for rehydration.
- Change state through behaviour methods named after the business action. No setters.
- Check invariants inside the aggregate and throw an unchecked exception from the `domain` package.
- Register domain events in the aggregate; flows publish them after saving via `pullDomainEvents()`.
- Value objects are records that validate in the compact constructor.
- Domain tests are plain JUnit and AssertJ, without a Spring context.
```

The header between the two `---` lines is YAML frontmatter: `paths` lists globs relative to the project root, and the rule loads only when Claude reads or edits a file that matches one, not at session start.

Add two more rules the same way:

- `data-and-migrations.md` with `paths: ["bookstore/src/main/java/**/data/**", "bookstore/src/main/resources/db/**"]`
- `testing.md` with `paths: ["**/src/test/**"]`

:::tip[Checkpoint]

Ask Claude to change a class under `domain`, and check that it follows the rule:

```text
Let a book's title be corrected after it was added to the catalogue.
```

Expected: Claude adds a method named after the business action (for example `correctTitle`), not a `setTitle`, as `domain.md` says: "Change state through behaviour methods named after the business action. No setters." A path-scoped rule loads when Claude reads or edits a file that matches its `paths`: when it opens `Book.java`, the rule is loaded. You do not have to keep the change.

:::

### 4. Explore the hierarchy

Add a conflicting line to your user file `~/.claude/CLAUDE.md` (for example "Always use Lombok") and a personal `CLAUDE.local.md` (for example your preferred commit style). Start a new session and run `/memory` and `/context`.

Observe: files are concatenated, not overridden. When instructions conflict, the agent has to choose. Remove the Lombok line afterwards.

### 5. Watch for context rot

Run `/context` and read the grid. The grid below is an illustration, not a screenshot: your symbols, names and numbers differ. Each symbol stands for 2k tokens of a 200k window.

```text
Context usage: 100k of 200k tokens (50%)

S S S S S S S C K M      S  System prompt                  14k   (7%)
M M M M M M M M M M      C  CLAUDE.md and auto memory       2k   (1%)
M M M M M M M M M M      K  Skill descriptions              2k   (1%)
M M M M M M M M M M      M  Messages and tool results      82k  (41%)
M M M M M M M M M M      .  Free space                    100k  (50%)
. . . . . . . . . .
. . . . . . . . . .
. . . . . . . . . .
. . . . . . . . . .
. . . . . . . . . .
```

What to watch for:

- S, C and K load before you type anything. Every line of your `CLAUDE.md` costs attention on every task.
- M grows with every prompt, answer, file read and tool result. It is the part you control.
- Free space shrinks as M grows. Long before it reaches zero, answers drift: that is context rot.
- Same task but drifting: `/compact focus on <topic>`. Unrelated next task: `/clear`.

Learn the three moves:

- `/clear` between unrelated tasks.
- `/compact focus on <topic>` when a long task drifts.
- Delegate big reads to a subagent ("use a subagent to find every place that formats money") so only the summary lands in your context.

A 1M-token window is not an attention guarantee.

### 6. Redo the Level 0 task

Redo the Level 0 task in a fresh session (`/clear`). Compare with your [observation card](./01-level-0-break-the-agent.mdx).

When you are done, compare your result with the branch [solution/level-1](https://github.com/MaikKingma/AgenticBookStore/tree/solution/level-1).

:::tip[Make it yours]

The best harness has a creative engineer who keeps tweaking it. Pick one thing Claude got wrong in Level 0 and write a rule of your own for it, for example `.claude/rules/acl.md` for the distributor client. Skills, hooks and commands of your own can follow in later levels. This is optional.

:::

## Before you move on

What improved, and what still went wrong even though it was written down? That last list is the bridge to Level 3: `CLAUDE.md` is guidance, the agent may decide a guideline does not apply.

## Level cleared when

- [ ] `CLAUDE.md` has at most 60 lines.
- [ ] Asked to change a class in `domain`, Claude follows `.claude/rules/domain.md`: a behaviour method named after the business action, no setters.
- [ ] In a fresh session, the Level 0 task ends with `./mvnw verify` green, shown in the terminal, and no ArchUnit violation.
